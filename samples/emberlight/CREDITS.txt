Emberlight's assets are original work for this sample.

- models/*.glb: built by build_models.py in Blender 4.5 from primitives; no third-party meshes,
  textures or materials.
- architecture.glb: built by build_scene.py from primitives; its embedded ashlar and slate maps
  are drawn by textures.py.
- scene.json, perception.json: written by build_scene.py.
- audio/*.wav: synthesized by build_audio.py, original.
- scripts/main.ts: game rules, original.

No downloaded art, audio or fonts are included. The engine's synthesized sound presets are used
through the project's [sounds] table.
