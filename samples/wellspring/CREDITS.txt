Wellspring: credits and licences

Original work (this repository's licence): the game rules (scripts/), the scene and its generators
(build_scene.py, terrain.py, scatter.py, sky.py), every building and prop modelled in
build_structures.py (sluice houses, gates, wheels, aqueduct, shrine, bell, raft, jetty, huts, well,
trough, jars, steles, cairns, handles, lanterns), the grass tufts and patches and the game-ready
conversions in build_models.py, the painted grass card and clouds (build_textures.py), the far
