## Folding and adjustments

Viewport rebuild-only source commits are folded into the change before them. A curated commit keeps
Pioneer's committed viewport package when Pioneer built it from that commit's viewport sources and
it names no local path; otherwise the package is rebuilt at that commit. Pioneer's packages from
`7b364031` on embed the builder's rustup library paths, which commit 3's build script remaps;
commits 20 to 23 rebuild theirs.

End trees differ from their source ends only where a later source commit corrects an earlier one
within reach, and where text must not be published:

- `b316b449`'s formatting of the play window is taken with commits 5 and 6, and `95e2f695`'s skip of
  the ripple step timing without GPU timestamps with commits 6 to 20.

  `fbd33620` (a round crown's orientation), `e21d853f` and `29b3d498` (the shadow cascades the water
  and the liquid surface sample under ray-traced shadows) later made to code it adds, and

  owner's own accounts.

  scope; from 3, the viewport path remap.

## Selection decisions and limits

- Thrown water: the spray emission (falls, landing, rapids, splashes, breaking lips, mist; at most
  64 splash and lip births a frame; parcels that end on landing), its ligament and drop drawing and
  the lip and fall ribbons stay the current implementation for everything except crowns and liquid
  particles. The owner judged the thrown-water layer the wrong direction (charter, 2026-10-23); the
  route the owner chose draws thrown water as one refracting liquid surface, which commit 20 does
  for liquid particles and entry crowns. The water's sound and foam impacts read the same emitters.
- Three-dimensional free-surface liquid stays out of the delivery scope; thrown water is
  presentation only and never enters the world, snapshots or replays.
- Under FXC no frame of a scene with a water region draws; a Windows host without DXC 1.8.2502 or
  newer runs Vulkan.
- Neural textures are beta: their tests are removed and their code stays.
- Implicit buoyancy and the wind sea bless the sailing scene's chain and layout folds again;
  version-1 Floaters and Sea give back the legacy chain.
- Recorded measurements keep their builds, machines and dates; liquids.md 21 and 22 measured
  `4913637d`, whose landing clouds this history does not contain.
- The known limits are those of the charter's frontier-water entry.
