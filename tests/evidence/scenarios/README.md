# Evidence: gameplay scenarios

Produced on 2026-09-19 with the debug (sanitized) build by `pocket scenario <sample> --seeds 3 --frames 900 --json` (`docs/design/scenarios.md`). Each file is the runner's report: per scenario the seeds run, passed and failed, ticks to pass, and per seed the verdict (`__scenario`: name, status, step, label, ticks, error) with the project's exposed values at the end of the run (the `__scenarios` name list is dropped from the files).

| File | Scenarios | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| `physics.json` | `samples/physics/scenarios/goal.ts` | "within eight seconds at least three bodies reach the goal" passes at every seed, in 173 to 203 ticks (the random drops differ per seed); "nothing falls out of the arena and the chain holds" passes after its six-second wait: the lowest body stays above the floor and every chain link is within 0.1 of its rest length; "the hatch drops to its stop and the paddle turns at its motor speed" (added with the hinge joints) passes after two seconds at every seed: `hatchAngle` within 0.05 of the 1.2 rad limit with `at_limit: 1`, `paddleSpeed` within 0.3 of the motor's 3 rad/s. |
| `sprites.json` | `samples/sprites/scenarios/coins.ts` | The platformer (`Body2D`, `docs/design/tilemaps.md`): walking right collects the coin ahead in 8 ticks; walking left for two seconds collects the three coins on the left (the scenario first claimed two: the runner reported "expected 2, got 3" at seed 1, 2 and 3, and the expectation was corrected, which is the kind of answer the tool exists for); the player stands grounded on solid ground and the west wall stops it at x > -10; a jump from below the ledge lands on it (`player.grounded` back to true above y -2) and collects the two coins over it in 66 ticks; the one-way plank is passed from below by a jump in place and landed on from above (rest at y -1) in 84 ticks. Every run is identical across seeds: the platformer has no randomness. |

`runtime_tests` (`[scenario]`) runs the sprites bundle in-process: the first scenario passes in under a second of simulated time, moving the coin out of reach makes it fail at "first coin" with "not within 1s", and an unknown scenario name quits at once.
