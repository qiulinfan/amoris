## The look (2026-10-02)

The owner asked for the editor's look to be made nicer (`docs/editor.md`, Look). Every picture here was taken on Windows 11 (Direct3D 12, the display at 200 percent, so a window of 960 by 540 points captures at 1920 by 1080 pixels) with the release runtime, before the change (`look-before*`, at 3bfe8506) and after it (`look-after*`). `look-before.png` and `look-after.png` come from `pocket editor physics --config release -- --hidden --frames 90 --capture <png> --json`; the others from `pocket editor <sample> --config release -- --hidden --serve <port> --paused [--size WxH]` driven over the control server (`pocket rpc --url http://127.0.0.1:<port> ui.click '{"id": "entity:Bowl"}'`, a short wait, `capture {"path": ...}`, `quit`).

- `look-before.png` / `look-after.png`: the physics sample at the default size, nothing selected.
- `look-before-selected.png` / `look-after-selected.png`: the same after a click on `entity:Bowl`: the hierarchy's selected row, the gizmo, the inspector filled (Transform, WorldTransform).
- `look-before-1600.png` / `look-after-1600.png`: `--size 1600x1000` after a click on `entity:Ramp`: the hierarchy with Log's children, the inspector down through MeshRenderer.
- `look-after-playing.png`: Bowl clicked, then `play`: the transport's Pause tinted in the accent and Stop's red square, the status bar green with the tick counting, the crates the project drops in the scene.
- `look-after-sprites.png`: the sprites sample at 1280 by 800 after `play`, `pause`, `entity:Level`, `layer:ground` and `tile:1`: the inspector's Tiles section with Stop painting, the picked layer and tile tinted, the status bar amber while paused.

What looked unfinished before, from the before pictures: the toolbar's ten buttons were all the same large bordered box, disabled ones hard to tell from enabled, with no grouping but gaps; the active tab of each dock was a solid accent block and every tab a bordered button; 1-pixel borders around every pane and 6-point splitters in a light grey cut the window into heavy frames; the hierarchy indented children by spaces and filled the selected row with the full accent; the inspector's labels and inputs did not line up (a single field was a 120-point input pushed to the right, a vector three 58-point inputs, a four-part rotation or colour ran past the pane's edge), component names were plain text with a large `remove` button, and nothing set one component apart from the next; the tick, entity count and hash shared the bottom tab row with a long notice and were cut off at the default size; the console was one undifferentiated line per entry; an empty inspector was a sentence in the corner; the view bar covered the project's HUD at the scene's top left (`samples/ui`, `samples/sprites`).

After: one palette, spacing scale and set of pieces at the top of `editor/main.tsx`; a 36-point toolbar with the brand, the transport as one group (Play in the accent, Pause tinted, Step and Stop with drawn icons), ghost buttons for history and entities, Save at the right beside the scene's file; flat tabs on a darker strip, the one in front joining its pane under an accent line, the hierarchy's count and the inspector's selection in fainter type; panes without borders, 4-point splitters in the window's colour that light up under the pointer; hierarchy rows of 22 points with a guide line per level, a square for a parent and a ring for a leaf, the primary selection stronger than the rest; inspector sections with a header bar (Remove, or `derived`), a label column and fields filling the rest, vectors and colours one input a part led by the axis letter in its colour, flags as check boxes, inputs sunken and outlined in the accent while focused; a status bar for the mode, the notice, tick, entities and hash; console lines in columns (tick when there is one, level in its colour, category, message); empty panes saying what is missing; the view bar at the scene's top right. `samples/ui`'s own interface keeps the SDK's colours (the editor sets `theme` in its own bundle only).

Checked: `./.pocket/pocket check` (no type errors) and `POCKET_ROOT=$PWD ./build/release/bin/runtime_tests.exe "[editor]"` (24 cases, 1609 assertions, all passing; at 1024 by 640 the `Ramp` row the tests click sits at y 372 to 394 of a hierarchy reaching 414, where it was at 420 to 445 of 434 before).
