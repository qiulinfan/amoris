# Terrain evidence

- `hills.png` (2026-09-29): `samples/hills` at 1280 by 720 after 30 ticks: noise hills 96 units across (seed 11, scale 36, five octaves, 12 high) meshed by the engine, grass on the gentle ground, rock on the slopes and snow over the tops blended from height and slope, the lake, the trees the script planted where `terrain.height` found gentle grass above the water (with their shadows), and the character standing on the shore.
- `editor-sculpt.png` (2026-09-29): `samples/hills` in the editor with the Hills selected and Sculpt on (the handles out of the way): three drags across the pane raised the ground where the camera's ray met it (revision 40), the whole valley under the scene's camera with its lakes, snow and rock.
- `scatter.png` (2026-09-29): the same view with the sample's two Scatters: 931 bushes on the gentle grass between the water and the snow and 362 stones leaning with the slopes (the rock too), 1293 copies drawn in 6 draw calls with their shadows.
- `path.png` (2026-09-29): the hills from the side of the dirt path the sample's script paints at its start (`terrain.paintPath`, 25 points from the player on the shore to the beacon on the peak, radius 1.6, coverage 0.6 a dab, colour 0.6, 0.47, 0.32): it winds up the valley in the ground's own light and shadow, and the bushes and stones leave it clear (`Scatter.max_paint` 0.3). Captured through the control server with the sample's camera turned off and one placed 20 units beside the path's middle, 12 above the ground there, looking at it; `terrain.info.painted` 0.013.
- `boulders.png` (2026-09-29): the hills' third Scatter, 60 boulders (spheres 1 by 0.75 by 0.9 at 1.4 to 2.6 times, sunk 0.35) on gentle ground above the water and off the path, each also a static capsule (`collide` 0.42, `collide_height` 0.8): `physics.stats.scattered` 60. Seen from 9 units off the densest cluster, 5 above it.
- `reeds.png` and `reeds-moved.png` (2026-09-29): the playground cleared and a Scatter of 1727 thin reeds on a floor (`sway` 0.35, `sway_speed` 0.6, `fade` 12), 1295 drawn: those past 12 units from the camera are left out, which is the empty far field. `reeds-moved.png` is `render.compare`'s diff after 20 ticks: every pixel that changed in red over the dimmed first frame (45% of the view), the reeds and their shadows swaying on the simulation clock.
- `layers.png` and `layers-off.png` (2026-09-29): the hills from behind the player at the start, looking up the path to the beacon, at 1280 by 720. With the sample's four textured layers (`Terrain.layers`: grass everywhere, tiled every 6 units; sand below 0.31 of the height, round the lake; rock on slopes over 32 degrees; dirt only where painted, which the script does along the path with `terrain.paintLayerPath`), each a seamless image made by `make_sample_assets.py --ground`; the player stands where the dirt's share is 0.78. `layers-off.png` is the same view with the layers removed: the vertex colours of grass, rock and snow they replace. The web build (`pocket pack hills --web --config wasm-small`) draws the same in the browser.

Reproduce: `./.pocket/pocket run hills -- --headless --frames 30 --json --capture hills.png --size 1280x720`, `./.pocket/pocket scenario hills`, `assets_tests "[terrain]"`, `runtime_tests "[terrain]"` (painting: `"[paint]"`; colliding copies: `"[collide]"`, and `physics_tests "[scatter]"`; swaying and fading: `renderer_tests "[sway]"`).
