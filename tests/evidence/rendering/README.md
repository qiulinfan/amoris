# Evidence: rendering (2026-09-19)

- `physics-shadows-90.png`: `./.pocket/pocket run physics -- --headless --frames 90 --capture ...` with the directional shadow map on (default). Crates and the ramp shadow the ground; `render.stats` reports `shadows: true` and one shadow-pass draw per instanced run.
- `tests/renderer_tests/renderer_tests.cpp` (`[shadows]`) builds a ground, a floating cube and a 45-degree sun from commands, captures the frame, and checks that the ground under the cube's shadow is darker than open ground, then that both match with `render.shadows {enabled: false}`.
- Instancing numbers are in `docs/evidence/swarm.md`.
- `msaa.png`: `./.pocket/pocket run assets -- --headless --frames 60 --size 960x540 --capture ...` with the sample's `[render] msaa = 4`. `tests/renderer_tests/renderer_tests.cpp` (`[msaa]`) counts hard transitions between neighbouring pixels of a playground frame at 256x144: 198 at one sample, 135 at four, while `render.pick` still names `/Level/Player` under its projection and `render.stats` reports `msaa: 4` with the id pass's own draws.
- `animation-layers.png`: the assets sample's arm at 0.5 s with `nod` as the base clip (the root tilted 20 degrees about X on its way to 30) and `wave` layered on with `mask: "tip"` (the tip swung 45 degrees about Z), captured through the runtime's JSON-RPC (`animation.play`, `animation.layer`, `step {ticks: 30}`, `capture`). `tests/runtime_tests/runtime_tests.cpp` (`[layers]`) checks the numbers: a mask of `tip` keeps `nod` from moving the root, `root` lets it through at 30 degrees, weight 0.5 gives 15, and an additive `nod` on top of `nod` gives 60.
