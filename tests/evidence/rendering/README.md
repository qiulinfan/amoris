- `physics-shadows-90.png`: `./.pocket/pocket run physics -- --headless --frames 90 --capture ...` with the directional shadow map on (default). Crates and the ramp shadow the ground; `render.stats` reports `shadows: true` and one shadow-pass draw per instanced run.
- `tests/renderer_tests/renderer_tests.cpp` (`[shadows]`) builds a ground, a floating cube and a 45-degree sun from commands, captures the frame, and checks that the ground under the cube's shadow is darker than open ground, then that both match with `render.shadows {enabled: false}`.
- Instancing numbers are in `docs/evidence/swarm.md`.
- `msaa.png`: `./.pocket/pocket run assets -- --headless --frames 60 --size 960x540 --capture ...` with the sample's `[render] msaa = 4`. `tests/renderer_tests/renderer_tests.cpp` (`[msaa]`) counts hard transitions between neighbouring pixels of a playground frame at 256x144: 198 at one sample, 135 at four, while `render.pick` still names `/Level/Player` under its projection and `render.stats` reports `msaa: 4` with the id pass's own draws.
- `animation-layers.png`: the assets sample's arm at 0.5 s with `nod` as the base clip (the root tilted 20 degrees about X on its way to 30) and `wave` layered on with `mask: "tip"` (the tip swung 45 degrees about Z), captured through the runtime's JSON-RPC (`animation.play`, `animation.layer`, `step {ticks: 30}`, `capture`). `tests/runtime_tests/runtime_tests.cpp` (`[layers]`) checks the numbers: a mask of `tip` keeps `nod` from moving the root, `root` lets it through at 30 degrees, weight 0.5 gives 15, and an additive `nod` on top of `nod` gives 60.

## Morph targets and root motion

`morph-root.png` was captured on 2026-09-19 through the JSON-RPC server (`pocket_runtime --project samples/assets --headless --serve 0 --paused --size 960x540`; `step 31`, `capture`), the assets sample at 0.517 s. The pink arm on the right is the `Pulse` entity playing the `pulse` clip, whose `weights` track has the `bulge` target at `0.967` (`pulse.bulge` in the state): the ring halfway up the arm is pushed out to twice its radius, on the GPU from the asset's deltas (`render.stats.morphed 1`). The arm behind the checkered crate on the left is the `Walker` on its `walk` clip with `root_motion 1`: its root travels one unit per second along its +Z and the engine moves the entity by it (`walker.z -0.983`, from -1.5), while the pose keeps the root joint at the entity, so the mesh stays with its transform; the sample turns it around every three seconds. The orange arm in front is the original `Arm` on its `wave` clip. `runtime_tests` (`[morph]`, `[root]`) check the weights, the leaned vertices through the id buffer, the travel per tick, the loop's wrap and the reporting mode (`docs/design/animation.md`).


## Inverse kinematics, look-at and root rotation

`ik.png` is `./.pocket/pocket run assets -- --headless --frames 200 --size 960x540 --capture ...` on 2026-09-19, the assets sample at 3.33 s. At the right the `Reacher`, an arm with an `IK` component (`end: tip`, two bones, the effector one unit up the tip joint), is bent at its elbow to the circling `Orb` (`reacher.error 0.001`, `reacher.reached true` in the state); behind it the `Gazer` aims its tip at the same Orb through a `LookAt` and has turned its full limit (`gazer.angle 70`). At the far left the `Turner` plays the arm's new `turn` clip with `root_motion 1` and `root_rotation` on: a quarter circle of radius one and a 90-degree turn per second, so after 3.33 s it has turned 300 degrees (`turner.yaw 300`) and walked most of its circle. `runtime_tests` check the numbers: `[ik]` a reachable target hit within a millimetre with both bones kept at unit length, an unreachable one stretched toward with the shortfall as the error, the elbow swung to either side of a pole entity, weight 0 leaving the pose, a target entity followed from a moved and turned arm, and the sample's Reacher within reach of the Orb most of the time; `[lookat]` a 90-degree turn in place, a 30-degree limit and half a weight; `[rotation]` the Turner's arc: (1, 0, 1) facing +X after a second, (2, 0, 0) after two, the start after four, across the clip's wraps, and mode 2 reporting `root_delta_yaw` without turning the entity.
